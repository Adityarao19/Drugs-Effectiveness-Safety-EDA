# 💊 Drugs Effectiveness & Safety EDA

A comprehensive EDA project focused on analyzing patient reviews of various drugs for different medical conditions. The goal is to explore the effectiveness, reported side effects, and sentiment expressed in reviews.

## 📊 Key Objectives
- Identify top-rated drugs and their associated conditions
- Analyze side effects and their frequencies
- Visualize sentiment trends in patient reviews
- Explore relationships between condition, rating, and usefulness

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- Python
- Pandas
- Plotly
- WordCloud
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📁 Dataset
Source: [Drug Review Dataset - Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jessicali9530/drug-reviews)
